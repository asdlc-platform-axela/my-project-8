- p.1 instructions: submit within 14 days of the accident; make no promise or payment to a third party and forward any third-party claim to the insurer immediately; acceptance of the form is not admission of liability; a police report is needed only when a third party is involved. → F1
- p.1 §01 Insured: full name, postal address, contact phone/fax, bank name and account number for crediting the approved claim, with a certified copy of the bank account page. → F1
- p.1 §02 Ownership: legal owner's name and address; the lease/hire-purchase/loan institution's name if the vehicle is financed. → F1
- p.1 §03 Driver: full name, age, relationship to the insured, driving-licence number with a copy attached. → F1
- p.1 §04 Accident details: date, time, location, the police station it was reported to, and a free-text description of what happened. → F1
- p.2 §05 Usage: what the vehicle was being used for at the time (private, hire, self-drive rental, driving tuition, other). → F1
- p.2 §06 Damage to goods carried: description and value of the damage. → F1
- p.2 §07 Injured passengers/driver: names, addresses, age, occupation, injury details. → F1
- p.2 §08 Third-party bodily injuries and third-party property damage, including the other party's insurer name and policy number. → F1
- p.2 §09 Other insurance covering the same vehicle or goods: yes/no, with details. → F1
- p.2 Declaration: the claimant attests the particulars are true, agrees to the insurer's privacy policy, and signs and dates the form. → F1
- p.3 Letter of Indemnity and Discharge Receipt — settlement-stage documents signed once a claim is approved and paid. → not used: insurer-side settlement paperwork, out of scope for this self-service submission (see prd.md Out of Scope)
