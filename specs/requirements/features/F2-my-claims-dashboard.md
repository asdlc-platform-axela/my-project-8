# My Claims Dashboard

## Purpose

Lets the signed-in claimant see every claim they have submitted, each shown simply as "Submitted" with its date.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F2.1 As a claimant, I see my submitted claims in one list, newest first, each with its date.
- F2.2 As a claimant, I see my draft claims in a separate list, so I can tell them apart from my submitted ones.
- F2.3 As a claimant, I resume a draft claim from the dashboard to finish and submit it.
- F2.4 As a claimant, I open a submitted claim to see everything I filled in, read-only.

## Decisions

- The dashboard shows two lists: drafts and submitted claims, clearly separated.
- A submitted claim's detail view is read-only, matching F1's rule that a submitted claim can no longer be changed by the claimant.
